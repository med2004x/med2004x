### Featured Projects

#### Mahall — Global Ecommerce Infrastructure
A multi-tenant SaaS platform that acts as complete infrastructure for independent digital commerce.
*   **The Problem**: Businesses need control over themes, seller onboarding, gating, and media management without being locked into restrictive marketplaces or complex self-managed infrastructure.
*   **The Solution**: A high-performance, multi-tenant SaaS that provides store management, plan/feature gating, media management, and admin workflows natively.
*   **Architecture**: Built as a distributed multi-tenant platform utilizing domain routing, strict tenant isolation, and caching layers to guarantee low latency.
*   **Technology Stack**: Go, PostgreSQL, Redis, Next.js, Docker, Cloudflare, R2 Storage.
*   **Business Impact**: Lowers the barrier to entry for businesses requiring bespoke ecommerce setups, providing the reliability of an enterprise platform at fraction of the cost.

#### TARDOC — Medical Billing SaaS
An operational automation tool designed for medical billing and healthcare administration.
*   **The Problem**: Healthcare providers lose significant resources and time navigating manual, slow, and error-prone billing workflows.
*   **The Solution**: An automated medical billing system that manages, validates, and tracks billing cycles directly, reducing administrative overhead.
*   **Architecture**: Event-driven transaction processing, structured billing pipelines, and secure storage for sensitive data.
*   **Technology Stack**: Python, Go, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Cloudflare.
*   **Business Impact**: Drastically reduces claim processing errors and admin hours, allowing healthcare offices to reallocate personnel to patient care.

### Engineering Philosophy

*   **Simplicity Over Complexity**: Code is a liability; systems should be as simple as possible to solve the business need. Over-engineering is an expensive distraction.
*   **Product-First Engineering**: Technology exists to power products and serve users. Code should be written with a deep understanding of product viability and market dynamics.
*   **Operational Thinking**: A system is only as good as its observability, maintainability, and deployment predictability. If you build it, you operate it.
*   **Ownership Mentality**: Autonomy requires taking absolute responsibility for the end-to-end outcome of a project—from design choices to system uptime.
